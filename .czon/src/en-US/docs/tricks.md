# Usage Tips

## Declaring Dates in the Document Body

You can use date declarations like "Today is January 8, 2026" in the document body (free format style), and CZON will automatically recognize and process it.

Traditional Markdown documents declare dates through YAML Frontmatter, requiring users to remember the corresponding fields; otherwise, dates may not be correctly identified.

CZON supports directly declaring dates within the document body, avoiding the problem of forgetting to fill in Frontmatter fields.

AI automatically extracts and infers date information from the document body and processes it as the document's creation date.

This method is more flexible and intelligent, enhancing the user experience.

As a side note, documents with dates can generate timeline views, making browsing and management easier. This is particularly suitable for content types like diaries and blogs that benefit from a timeline.
